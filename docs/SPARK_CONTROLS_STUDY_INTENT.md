# Spark authoring controls study intent

Written before the control comparison renders. This is a bounded authoring-capability
study, not a new fire body, gas formula, completed phoenix or accepted initial artwork.

## Intended meaning and comparison

An existing burning plume sheds rising incandescent grains, or an existing fire dash
sheds grains from its moving rear surface; the grains coast, cool and disappear after
the source stops. Authors can set the number of births independently from spark glow.
The primary silhouette, trail, residue, motion laws, palette and timing stay unchanged.
Compare omitted controls, explicit existing defaults, zero births, lower/higher counts,
and zero/normal/stronger spark glow on the same supported plan and seed.

## Form, force and material

- Model: stylized-physical analytic drag and gravity, not a fluid simulation.
- Silhouette: preserve the existing plume or dash body; compact detached grains are
  secondary. They retain their existing short exposure traces and front/back layers.
- Source and direction: upward from the plume, or the dash's moving rear surface with
  inherited source velocity. Birth locations follow the existing source schedule.
- Material and weight: incandescent grains with size-dependent drag and downward
  acceleration. Light strength changes only their glow, never their matter opacity.
- Causal chain: the existing source emits finite births; those births travel and cool;
  emission stops at the plan's existing sourceEmissionEnd, then grains finish their lives.
- Timing: preparation, release, travel and response use each plan's existing times;
  there is no added impact. Exact endpoints stay empty within the declared duration.

## Likely misreads and review limits

More sparks or stronger glow can obscure the primary silhouette or clip highlights.
Sparse grains may read as noise; the unchanged primary can still read as leaves,
noodles, armor or broad flat orange patches. Controls do not solve those defects.
The user wants vivid Niagara-like results with crisp clusters; this study neither
promises Niagara equivalence nor claims that this initial-art quality target is met.

Check actual native bounds at count 720 without silently changing speed, width, life,
canvas or motion. Inspect native-size comparisons on the intended dark background;
other backgrounds are diagnostic. The parent owns final comparison renders and cost.
No normal-speed playback has been viewed and no user artwork approval is recorded.
Automated tests establish API/routing/preservation properties, not aesthetic quality.
